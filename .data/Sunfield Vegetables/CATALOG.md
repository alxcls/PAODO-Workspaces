# Sunfield Vegetables — Wholesale Catalog

Sun-grown bell peppers and fresh basil, harvested and packed to order.
Batch size: 2,000 g bell pepper per harvest run, or 500 leaves basil per harvest run.

---

## Products

Prices and units are in `data/catalog.json`.

---

## How to Order

Read `data/catalog.json` for prices. Read `data/inventory.json` for live stock.

To buy: `python scripts/sell_stock.py <ingredient> <quantity> "<your name>"`

Each product is harvested separately — ask us to harvest whichever is low.
